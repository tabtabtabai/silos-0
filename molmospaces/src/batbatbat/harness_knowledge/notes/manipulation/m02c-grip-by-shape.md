Long items: grip the shaft or handle across, near the heavy end. Round items: through the centre, at or just below the widest part. Hollow items: a handle, or one wall with one finger inside the opening, never across the whole body; if one is lying down, use a handle that stands off the body, or tilt the hand 40 to 60 degrees and pinch the wall at the opening. Tipped items: grip what is narrowest now. If no part fits between the open fingers with about 1.5 cm to spare, do not pinch knobs or tufts: push it onto a flat destination with closed fingers below its centre height, in short pushes, or leave it for last. Close square to the narrow dimension, within about 15 degrees.
Why: a close across a wide curved body slides off on the lift.
