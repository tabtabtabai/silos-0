Long items (tools, rods, sticks): grip the shaft or handle across, near the balance point; with a heavy end that is near the heavy end, not the middle of the outline. Round items: through the centre, at or just below the widest part. Items wider than the open gripper: a rim, edge, handle or neck that fits, or the narrowest side. Tipped or lying items: grip what is narrowest now, not what would be narrowest upright. Close square to the narrow dimension, within about 15 degrees.
Why: a grip off the balance point turns or slides out when lifted, and one along the long axis has nothing to squeeze.
