Before reaching, check the room each finger needs beside the item, not only above it. Choose the closing direction so both fingers go down into gaps, between item and wall or between neighbours. If no direction fits, tilt the hand away from the nearest wall, grip a part that sticks up above the rim, or first push the item or a neighbour to open a gap. Lift an item out of a holder straight up until it clears the rim before moving sideways.
Why: the motion tools do not avoid collisions, and a finger landing on a wall or neighbour stops the grasp before it starts.
