A reading near 0 right after closing may still be a hold: it lags, and an item as wide as the open hand reads near 0. Decide by a short lift, not by reopening at once. The finger gap is about 8.5 x (1 - closure) cm; if that is much narrower than the item's body, you hold a cap, neck or edge, so set it down and re-grip across the body below its middle before carrying. Release an item as wide as the open hand while it still hangs 1-2 cm above the surface, not pressed onto it.
Why: the closure alone misleads in both directions, and an item wedged between open pads stays with the hand.
