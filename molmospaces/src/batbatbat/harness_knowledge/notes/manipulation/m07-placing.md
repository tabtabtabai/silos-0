Carry the item over its destination above everything in between, lower until just above or touching the surface, and open. Into a container, lower inside over the middle of the opening first. An item inside its container is placed: do not be a perfectionist about it; move on. Onto an item or stack, line up the centres. Onto a raised surface, rise above it before moving over. Read relations (in front of, behind, beside, centre) from the viewpoint facing the scene, and leave a margin of about half the item's width so the relation plainly holds. Upright: set it on its base and open only once it rests level. Lift straight up, then look; if it landed outside or fell over, pick it up and place it again.
Why: an item dropped from height bounces, rolls or tips out of its destination.
