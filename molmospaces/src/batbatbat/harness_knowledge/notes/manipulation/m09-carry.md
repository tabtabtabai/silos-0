Items held off their centre (by a handle, rim, knob or one end) swing: keep the wrist as you grasped until low over the destination, and travel in separate moves of about 0.3 m or less. After each carry move, check the item is still between the fingers (closure not moved toward fully closed, the item in the hand in the image) before releasing; if it fell, find where it landed and grip it from its new pose.
Why: an item lost on the way is only noticed at the destination unless you check, and a long fast move or wrist turn shakes an off-centre grip loose.
