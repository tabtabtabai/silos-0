If a container's opening faces sideways, measure its upper edge first. Only the fingers and the held item go under that edge: keep the wrist and gripper body outside, with the fingers tilted about 45 degrees toward the opening or the item hanging just below the fingertips. Advance until the item is about half its length inside, then release. Pushing it along the support or driving the wrist into the edge does not get it in.
Why: a wrist that enters the opening hits its upper edge and shoves the container away.
