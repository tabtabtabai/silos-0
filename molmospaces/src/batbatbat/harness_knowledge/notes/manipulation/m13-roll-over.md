A round item wider than the open hand with no stem, handle or rim to grip cannot be pinched. Roll it: close the gripper, fingers down, closing direction across the path, set explicitly. Rest the fingertips on its top behind centre, press lightly and move; it travels half as far as the hand. Keep its path at least its width from table edges; if it starts near one, roll it inward first. Move about its width per leg, then re-locate. At a raised edge, put the hand's wide side against its back, fingertips at a fifth of its height, press it a quarter of its width into the edge, then lift by its own height while moving a third of its width forward. If it falls back, repeat lower.
Why: a grippy ball rolls under a light press, a ball that reaches a table edge is lost, and an edge acts as a hinge when it is lifted from behind.
