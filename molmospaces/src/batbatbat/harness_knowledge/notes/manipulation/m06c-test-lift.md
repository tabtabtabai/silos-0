Close, then lift a short distance in its own move and confirm the item rose with the hand (closure short of fully closed, the item clear of its support in the image) before any long move. On a heavy item, wait one step after closing: if closure settled below about 0.2, or kept rising while the arm was still, the grip will slip, so open and grip a narrower part nearer the heavy end. Never close, lift high and carry in one path. A longer duration does not make a move gentler; only shorter moves do.
Why: a failed grasp discovered at the destination wastes the whole carry, and grips near the open limit slip in the first few centimetres.
