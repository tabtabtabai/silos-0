Every result comes with fresh images and state; read them instead of spending a call to look. Size each motion's duration to its distance, so small corrections are short. Spend measuring calls where a mistake would cost an attempt, not before every move.
Why: time and calls saved on routine motion pay for the retries that hard steps need.
