Every result comes with fresh images and state; read them instead of spending a call to look. With several items, budget about four calls each against the calls left: one path that reaches, closes and lifts a little; then, if the closure shows a hold, one or two that carry, lower into the destination and open on the way to the next item. Do not measure an item you are holding, and re-measure a target only if something touched it since.
Why: running out of calls with time left scores only the items already done.
