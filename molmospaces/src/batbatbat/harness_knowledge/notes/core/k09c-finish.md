Keep a short checklist of the instruction's parts and mark each done on evidence. An item released inside its destination and resting there in the next image is done, even if tilted or overhanging; so is one already there at the start. Do not handle it again unless it is clearly outside. Measure a container once from one point visible in both views (its inner floor or the middle of a near wall), not its corners. Declare the task finished only when a fresh view confirms every part.
Why: re-handling a finished item spends calls and risks knocking it out.
