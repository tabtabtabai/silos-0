title: Diagnose before retrying, then change one cause
layer: core
status: active

evidence:
  - "Manipulation guidance used by the submitted policy."
confidence: measured
falsify: "Retry success after a diagnosed change is no better than an identical retry on the validation split."
---
After a failure, first name what went wrong from the evidence: missed, closed on nothing, closed on the wrong thing, slipped, blocked by something, unreachable, or a delegated policy stalled. Then: (1) correct that cause by a clear amount, a few centimetres or a new spot, not a nudge; (2) if the same symptom returns, the correction was too small or the approach is wrong: change direction, the part you grip, orientation, or the tool; (3) after a second failure of that approach, switch strategy or do another part and come back. A failed attempt often moves the item, so measure it again first. Later attempts still often succeed; keep going while budget remains. Repeat an identical call only for a tool that is random by design, and only once.
Why: a second try without a new reason usually fails for the same reason as the first.
