After the test lift, look at how the item hangs: if it tilts, turns or swings in the fingers, the grip is off balance; set it down and re-grip nearer the heavy end or the middle of its mass. Turn the wrist before grasping or after releasing, not while carrying a long or heavy item. Carry low over clear space, at a moderate speed, without sharp reversals. After each carry move, check the item is still between the fingers (closure has not moved toward fully closed, the item is in the hand in the image) before releasing; if it fell, find where it landed, measure it again and grip it from its new pose.
Why: an item lost on the way is only noticed at the destination unless you check, and releasing an empty hand wastes the whole carry.
