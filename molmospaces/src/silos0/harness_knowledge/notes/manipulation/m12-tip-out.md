Only for an item that must end up lower or beside where it is, such as out of a container onto the surface below. If it is tall with its weight high up, or a pinch on it has already slipped, do not lift it again: tip it out. Find the container's lowest side with table beyond it, never toward a table edge. Close the gripper, tilt the fingertips about 30 degrees down and away from you, set them on its far side a fifth of its height below its top, and push toward the low side by about its height in one firm move so it topples. If it drapes over the edge, push the part still inside, low, half its height further until it falls out.
Why: a heavy or top-heavy item twists out of a pinch, but pushed high it tips over, and a low edge lets it fall out.
