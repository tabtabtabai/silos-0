Carry the item over its destination above everything in between, lower until just above or touching the surface, and open. Into a container, lower inside over the middle of the opening. Onto an item or stack, line up the centres. If the item hangs off-centre from the hand (held by a handle, rim or one end), line up the item, not the hand: shift by the offset from the fingertips to its middle, and lower until its bottom, not the fingertips, is just above the target. Read relations from the viewpoint facing the scene, with a margin of about half the item's width. Lift straight up, then look; if it landed outside or fell over, place it again.
Why: an item dropped from height bounces or tips out, and lining up the hand leaves an off-centre item a radius off target.
