Close with the finger pads overlapping the part you grip by most of their length. For anything lower than the pads are tall, bring the fingertips down to just clear of its support; for taller items, put the fingertips below the middle of the part, not level with its top. Judge this from the fingertip height against the object's top and its support, not from the mount height. Just before closing, check the newest image: the named item, centred between the open fingers; if not, correct first.
Why: closing near the top catches an edge or nothing, the commonest single cause of a missed grasp.
