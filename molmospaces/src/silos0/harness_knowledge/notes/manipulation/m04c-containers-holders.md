Before reaching, check the room each finger needs beside the item, not only above it; choose the closing direction so both fingers go into gaps, tilt away from the nearest wall, or first push the item to open a gap. A container wall that is lower on one side is the way out. To take a long or heavy item out of a holder, do not lift its free end straight up: grip it, then draw it toward you along its length while rising about 1 cm per 3 cm, so the heavy end slides over the lowest wall. Lift a light item straight up until it clears the rim before moving sideways.
Why: a free end lifted straight up hangs the whole weight on a short lever and twists out of the pads.
