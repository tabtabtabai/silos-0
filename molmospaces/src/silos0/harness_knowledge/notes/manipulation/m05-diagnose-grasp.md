Judge by where the fingertips were against the item's measured top, middle and centre, and by the lift, not by closure alone. Item unmoved, fingertips above its middle: too high; go at least 2 cm lower. Fingertips beside it: re-measure its centre from closer. Pushed or tipped before closing: a finger hit it on the way down; approach higher with the fingers aligned to the gaps, from the side with more room. Partly closed but the item stayed down when lifted: the grip was shallow, on a wide or slippery part, or far from the balance point; grip deeper on a narrower part nearer it. Swung when lifted: off balance; re-grip nearer the balance point. Stopped by contact above grip height: find what was hit, then change the approach.
Why: each symptom points to one cause, and a retry that changes that cause is the one likely to work.
