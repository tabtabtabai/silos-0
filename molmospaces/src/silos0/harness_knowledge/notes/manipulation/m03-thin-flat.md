An item thinner than the pads are tall gives the fingers little to hold from above. Lower the open fingers until they touch the surface, rise a few millimetres, then close across the item's narrowest width. If that fails, change the item's pose rather than repeating the grip: push it until an edge overhangs the surface edge or rests against something, or press one end to raise the other, then grip the raised part.
Why: a flat item lies below where most grips close, and repeating the same close fails the same way.
