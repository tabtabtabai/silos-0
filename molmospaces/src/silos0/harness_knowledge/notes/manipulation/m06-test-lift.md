After closing, lift a short distance and confirm the item rose with the hand (closure short of fully closed, load present, the item clear of its support in the image) before any long move. If it stayed down, it is a missed grasp: diagnose it before trying again.
Why: discovering a failed grasp at the destination wastes the whole carry.
